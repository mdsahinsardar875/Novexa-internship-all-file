About Me

I'm a final-year B.Tech CSE student who likes building things that actually work end to end — from a styled button in the browser down to the query that fetches its data. My days split between two worlds: shipping full stack apps with the MERN stack, and training and evaluating AI systems where precision and clear reasoning matter more than speed.

I care about writing code that's easy to read a year later, debugging methodically instead of guessing, and explaining technical decisions in plain language. Outside of coursework, I organize class events, captain my cricket team, and keep showing up to coding challenges — mostly because I like the process of getting slightly better each time.
